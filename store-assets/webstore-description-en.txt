Your browsing data, gone with one click.

Your browser's own "Clear browsing data" dialog asks the same questions every time. One Click History Cleaner remembers your answers. Choose once what should be removed, and from then on a single click on the toolbar icon is all it takes.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

⚡ WHAT IT DOES

• One click. No menus, no dialog, no confirmation.
• You decide what goes. Ten data types, each with its own checkbox.
• Keep the sites you need. Add sites like github.com to the exclusion list to keep their cookies, site data and history, so you stay logged in. A site covers its subdomains too, so google.com also keeps mail.google.com.
• Tabs the way you want. Reload the current tab, all tabs, all but the current one, or close every tab for a clean start.
• One short notification tells you when it's done.
• Settings save instantly and sync with your browser account.
• Light and dark: the settings page follows your system theme.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🧹 WHAT CAN BE CLEANED

History
• Browsing history: pages you have visited
• Downloads: the list of downloads (the files themselves stay)
• Form data: autofill entries such as addresses

Cookies & site data
• Cookies: where websites keep your logins
• Local storage, IndexedDB and file systems: data websites store in your browser

Cache
• Browser cache: images, scripts and other downloaded resources
• Cache storage and service workers

Saved passwords, bookmarks and extensions are never touched.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN

• Runs entirely on your device. It sends nothing anywhere and collects no data.
• No access to the websites you visit.
• Access to your history is optional and only asked for when you add the first site to keep, so their history entries can stay.
• All of the code is open source: https://github.com/firsttris/oneclickhistorycleaner

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

🚀 HOW TO USE

1. Pin the extension to your toolbar.
2. Click the icon. Your browsing data is removed right away. Everything is selected by default.
3. To change what gets removed, or which sites to keep, right-click the icon and choose Options.
